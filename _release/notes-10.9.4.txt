The crash that stopped the bot while upgrading the Grand Warden ("Variable subscript badly formatted") was fixed for the six main village heroes in 10.8.10; the same weakness was still there in the builder base. The levels of the Battle Machine, the Battle Copter, the Double Cannon, the Archer Tower, the Multi Mortar and the cannons are read by OCR on the info bar, and were used straight away as an index of their cost table: an empty read, or one bad digit turning level 3 into 35, killed the bot the same way. All 40 cost lookups of the bot now go through one guarded accessor: an unusable level gives a cost of zero, the game refuses the upgrade itself, and the bot carries on instead of stopping.
