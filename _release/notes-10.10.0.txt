New: MultiBot. MultiBot.exe, next to the bot, runs several villages at once: one bot per profile, each on its own BlueStacks instance. A setup holds the profile, the instance (listed from BlueStacks) and the launch options, plus the settings of that profile that matter with several bots: window arrangement and offsets, screen capture mode, dedicated ADB port, processor cores, threads, how many bots may attack at the same time, and the Halt attack condition. Start all or close all with one button, or per setup with the right click: start, close, restart the bot, but also start the run, stop it, pause and resume without closing it, and start or stop the instance. A State column follows every bot (Off, Starting, Idle, Running, Paused). Shortcuts on the Desktop or in the Startup folder start a setup by itself. Bots are closed through their own interface, as their Exit button does, so settings are saved and no Watchdog relaunches them. Derived from the MultiBot of MyBot.run, rewritten for this version. See the README.
