Walls work at any level again: when the image search finds nothing and the builder menu is used instead, the game picks the wall itself and it can be of any level. The bot used to give up unless that wall matched the level of the Village > Upgrade combo; it now follows the level the game offers, moves the combo to it and recomputes the price of one wall, so the batch upgrade stays correct. The level switch no longer goes through the GUI combo, so it also works in mini and GUI-less mode.
The bot's own wall templates (imgcv\own\Walls\<level>\) stay optional per level: a level with no template folder is only a debug line in the log, not an error.
