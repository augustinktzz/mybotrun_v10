Fix: the Discord status now follows the Application ID and the invite as soon as you leave their box, whether the bot is running or not. Until now the two boxes were only read when the settings were saved, so pasting the ID and expecting the status to appear did nothing, and a stop/start or a profile switch was needed for the bot to see it. Changing the ID while connected reopens the connection with the new one; changing the invite or the Link box updates the button on the profile at the next refresh.
