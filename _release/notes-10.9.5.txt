New: Discord Rich Presence. Village > Notify > "Discord status" shows on your own Discord profile what the bot is doing - Town Hall level and profile name, gold, elixir and dark elixir, and the time the bot has been running - with an optional "Join the Discord" button under it. Off by default, and nothing leaves the machine: the bot talks to the Discord application through its local pipe and Discord publishes the status, so no webhook, no server, nothing to install. It needs the Discord application running on the same PC and a free Application ID created on discord.com/developers/applications, pasted next to the option; the README explains the three steps and how to add the pictures. The status is public, so the option stays off until you turn it on. The bot updates it at most every 15 seconds to respect the Discord rate limit, reconnects by itself if Discord is closed and reopened, and clears the status when you stop the bot.
